## 1. Executive Summary

Green Circle is Ethiopia's first open, founder-facing startup directory. It gathers the startups being built across the country — information that today sits scattered across hubs, demo days, and word of mouth — and maps it into one place, with due diligence and a clear readiness signal so investors can find and assess startups they would otherwise never see.

**The problem.** Ethiopia has a real, fast-growing startup economy and almost no way to see it. Tracked ecosystem value reached roughly **$303M in 2024**, and startup jobs grew from **85 in 2018 to 16,289 in 2025**, yet funding stays concentrated in a handful of visible firms — largest single round $42M, average round just $0.24M. Most startups doing real work are invisible until they are already obvious. Investors miss them; capital clusters; the ecosystem has no shared record.

**The opportunity.** Be the single, trusted place where Ethiopia's startups are listed, assessed, and discovered. We start as a directory with due diligence because it is concrete and ours to build first. Over time, Green Circle grows into a place where founders gather to build their startups and reach investors who trust our due diligence.

**Where we are.** A working platform is built. We are pre-revenue, pre-registration, and adding our first startups.

**The team.** Bemnet Mussa (founder — built the platform, leads product and strategy) and Alemayehu Dabi (co-founder — business development and partnerships), with Jaleta Kebede and Ezra Leye on development, and a business mentor advising.

**The ask.** An illustrative seed of **$30,000–$50,000** `[assumption — to finalize]` to fund ~18 months of lean operations and a first founder-support pilot. Use of funds and projections are in Section 8.

## 3. Market & Industry Analysis

**The industry.** Ethiopia's startup ecosystem is young but rising fast. Per the RISA Fund / GrowthAfrica & Systemic Innovation report (2024):

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Startups tracked in the ecosystem | 426 |
| Share with some funding | ~90% |
| Total ecosystem value (2024) | ~$303M |
| Largest single funding round | $42M |
| Average funding per startup | ~$0.24M |
| Startups holding patents | 2 (15 total) |
| Startup jobs created (2018–2025) | 85 → 16,289 |

Two facts define our opening. First, **the ecosystem is real and accelerating** — value and jobs are climbing steeply. Second, **value and visibility cluster in a few firms** — the $42M-vs-$0.24M gap shows most startups raise little or nothing, and the same report notes its data captures mainly investor-visible, formal firms and misses the early-stage majority. That invisible majority is precisely who Green Circle exists to surface.

**The demand side (our customers).** Local VCs, angels, diaspora backers, development-finance and impact funds, and banks with SME arms all want Ethiopian deal flow but lack a trusted, low-effort way to source it. Today they rely on demo days, personal networks, and luck.

**Market sizing (directional).**
- **TAM** — everyone who wants to discover or back Ethiopian startups: the full startup base, plus local, diaspora, and institutional capital and ecosystem partners.
- **SAM (Addis-first, v1)** — the active Addis startup base plus the investors, banks, and diaspora syndicates reachable in our first 12–18 months.
- **SOM (year 1)** — a first set of listed startups (~12) and a seeded demand side (~10 active investors), expanding steadily. `[assumption: refine with named counts]`

**Competition.**
- **Global signal platforms (e.g., Harmonic.ai)** — well-funded, closed, built for Western VCs; they do not cover Ethiopia.
- **Repositories (Crunchbase, PitchBook, Dealroom)** — broad, expensive, and thin or absent on early-stage Ethiopian firms.
- **Informal channels (WhatsApp, demo days, word of mouth)** — the real status quo: no coverage, no signal, no record.
- **Local hubs and accelerators** — partners, not competitors; they produce startups but offer no shared, investor-facing directory.

**Our position.** Open, founder-facing, local-first, with due diligence investors can trust. We differentiate on approach, not by inventing a new problem.

## 4. Product / Service Description

**The platform today.**
- **The directory** — Green Circle gathers startups from across Ethiopia and maps them into one searchable place: team, stage, traction, funding need.
- **Due diligence & Signal Score** — each startup gets a transparent 0–100 readiness signal, weighted **Profile 40 / Traction 30 / Demand 20 / Freshness 10**, built only from real data. This is the due-diligence layer investors rely on.
- **Investor surface** — ranked and filterable. Sort by signal, sector, or stage, and reach a startup directly.
- **The "Pulse" digest** — a periodic summary of who's moving, built from real signals, sent to investors and partners.

**What makes it credible.** Real data and a no-fabrication rule. No startup is listed without verification, and no number is shown that isn't measured.

**Roadmap.**
1. **Now — the directory and due diligence.** Startups gathered, mapped, verified, and scored.
2. **Next — deeper due diligence.** Our assessment record becomes a reference investors lean on before they commit.
3. **Later — founder support.** A place where founders gather to build their startups, supported by Green Circle and backed by investors who trust our due diligence. Resourced in stages, not built on day one.

## 5. Marketing & Sales Plan

We grow supply and demand deliberately, in order. Investor trust comes first.

**Phase 0 — Build the directory (now → launch).** Add the first **~12 startups, all real and verified**. Target: a launch surface an investor would screenshot and forward.

**Phase 1 — Seed demand (launch → +90 days).** Direct outreach to **20–30 named investors and partners** (Appendix B). Offer: free access to the directory and due diligence, plus the monthly Pulse. We sell *sourcing time saved*, not software.

**Phase 2 — Partnerships as supply + distribution (+90 → +180 days).** Sign hubs, accelerators, universities, and banks to feed verified startups and to distribute. Banks with SME arms are both a distribution channel (co-branded "rising startups") and a future revenue path.

**Phase 3 — The flywheel.** More listed startups → more investors show up → more founders want in because that's where investors are → richer signal data → better matching → repeat.

**Channels.**
- *Startups:* innovation hubs, university entrepreneurship programs, demo days, and direct referral.
- *Investors / banks / diaspora:* warm intros first (we already have paths into Addis Angels and advisory networks), then the Pulse digest as a nurture tool.

**Sales motion.** Concierge, not self-serve, in v1. One warm conversation beats twenty cold emails; we start with the relationships we already have.

## 6. Operational Plan

**How it works (our core daily work).**
1. **Source** — find a real startup via hubs, referrals, or direct contact.
2. **Verify** — confirm the startup and its traction are real.
3. **List** — add it to the directory: team, stage, traction, funding need.
4. **Assess** — generate the Signal Score from real inputs (the due-diligence step).
5. **Surface** — publish to the investor view and include in the next Pulse.
6. **Maintain** — freshness weighting decays stale listings.

**Cadence.** A steady stream of verified startups. Quality over count protects the directory's credibility and keeps a small team trustworthy against a faster, shallower competitor.

**Technology & hosting.** A web platform with a real analytics and Signal Score pipeline already built. Early-stage cloud hosting and tooling run at roughly **$30–80/month** `[assumption]`, scaling only as usage grows.

**Quality control.** The no-fabrication rule is operational, not aspirational: nothing is listed without a real startup behind it, and no metric is shown that isn't measured. This is the product's whole credibility, so it is enforced at every step.

**Later-stage operations.** A founder-support program and a shared space add real operational weight — selection, mentoring, a physical location. These are deferred until trust and capital justify them.

### 8.3 Revenue model (staged — follows trust, not the other way around)

We launch **free on both sides** to build liquidity. Candidate streams, in order of timing:

1. **Investor subscription** *(near-term)* — premium tools: advanced filters, signal alerts, export, market-intelligence dashboards. A generous free tier stays.
2. **Partner / bank sponsorship** *(near-term)* — hubs, banks, and funds pay to feature startups or sponsor the Pulse.
3. **Founder-support economics** *(later)* — equity or success-aligned facilitation on funded deals we source and vet. Designed so we never appear to sell access to founders.

**Guardrail:** no revenue stream may create pressure to inflate a signal. If it does, we don't ship it.
