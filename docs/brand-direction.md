# Green Circle — Brand Direction

**One-pager · Round 1 · October 2025 · Draft for Alemayehu & Bemnet**

---

## The direction, in one sentence

**Paper & Ink** — a warm, editorial, magazine-led identity that looks hand-made, reads long-form, and treats every founder as a profile subject rather than a data row.

## The feeling, stated plainly

> When someone opens Green Circle, they should feel the way they feel when they open a good Sunday magazine someone left on the coffee table. Considered. Unhurried. A little proud of itself, but quietly so. They should want to keep reading.

That is the whole design brief in one paragraph. Everything below is how we get there.

---

## What we are reacting against

The current site reads as Ministry-meets-SaaS-dashboard: emerald brand green, shield badges stamped on every card, fake numbers ("150+ Verified Startups / 500+ Active Global Investors"), a dark "Due Diligence, automated" panel with a fabricated bar chart. It performs authority. We want to stop performing and start publishing.

**We are not:** Crunchbase. A government portal. A fintech landing page. A compliance dashboard.

**We are:** a curated editorial home, run by two people who actually meet the founders, written like a magazine, designed like a small-batch quarterly.

**What the palette rejects:** emerald `#10b981` (it's every Web3 app), slate-900 as a compositional slab, indigo accent gradients, any color picked up from a generic Tailwind default.

**Contrast check:** ink on paper is 14.8:1. Ink-muted on paper is 7.3:1. Forest on paper is 8.9:1. All pass WCAG AA large and small. Ember on paper is 5.1:1 — fine for large text and UI, avoid for long-form body.

### The pairing

- **Display:** [Fraunces](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces) — variable serif, opsz + SOFT axes, available on Google Fonts. Warm, slightly expressive on large sizes, disappears cleanly at smaller sizes. Soft axis set to ~50 for a hand-pressed feel.
- **Body:** [Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter) — already loaded on the site. Kept. Proven long-form.
- **Mono (rare, hero only):** system mono (`ui-monospace`, `SFMono-Regular`, `Menlo`). Used once, for an "Issue 01 · October 2025" dateline on the hero-cover variant.
- **Future-proofing:** `Noto Sans Ethiopic` queued for Amharic bilingual lines. Not shipping in Round 1 but tokens include it as a fallback.

## Motifs & grammar

The visual grammar is deliberately short.

- **Hairline rules** over shadows. 1px, `--rule` color. Sections separate with a rule + whitespace, not a card.
- **The imperfect circle.** A single hand-drawn ink loop, ~350° arc (not closed — that's what makes it feel drawn rather than rendered). It lives in the logo, serves as a section divider, and appears in the `HandpickedMark` as the curatorial glyph.
- **Drop caps** on long-form paragraphs (opt-in class). Fraunces, 3-line drop, tucked into body text.
- **No gradients.** None. The old `bg-gradient-to-r from-primary to-teal-500` on the hero headline dies here.
- **Shadow budget:** `shadow-sm` only, and only where a card genuinely needs to lift off paper. Most cards won't use any — the rule handles separation.
- **Radius:** `4 / 8 / 12 / 20`. Nothing larger. Pill-shaped `rounded-3xl` cards are out. Buttons are `rounded-md` (8px), cards are `rounded-lg` (12px), the one hero photo is `rounded-2xl` (20px).
- **Asymmetric grids.** Editorial pages use a 12-column grid with prose pinned to columns 2–9, metadata columns 10–12. Not centered everything.
- **Whitespace budget.** Sections breathe at 96–128px top/bottom on desktop, 64–80px on mobile. If a section feels cramped, it is.

## Photography direction

- **Real Ethiopian subjects in Ethiopian contexts.** Offices above restaurants, workshops, enset fields, coffee cooperatives, streets of Addis / Bahir Dar / Hawassa. No generic Unsplash-business-handshake energy.
- **Warm grade** — amber lift in shadows, pulled-back digital blue, natural light preferred. The grade matters. A cold-blue founder shot kills the editorial feel instantly.
- **Aspect:** 3:4 portrait for founder shots, 16:9 for environment.
- **Credit every photograph** — photographer byline in the caption, styled with `--ink-faint`. It's editorial, not stock.
- **When there's no photograph** (Round 1 default, until Alemayehu & Bemnet send real ones): no silhouette avatars, no stock. A **typographic card** — initials in Fraunces display, on `--paper-deep`, with the hand-drawn ink circle quietly anchoring. It looks intentional rather than missing-image. A labeled Unsplash image used during staging will always be marked `{/* PLACEHOLDER PHOTO — swap before launch */}` in the code.

## The mark

The current wordmark has the right instinct — a hand-drawn circle lassoing "Circle" — but the SVG path is a lopsided quadratic curve that reads as a glitch rather than a pen stroke. The refresh:

- **The loop**: a proper ink-pen Bezier, ~350° arc so it doesn't close (a closed circle looks rendered; an open one looks drawn). Stroke width varies slightly along the path, with a pressure feel at the start.
- **The wordmark**: "Green" in Fraunces 500, small tracking. "Circle" the same, with the ink loop passing behind the "C" rather than wrapping the full word.
- **Standalone mark**: the ink circle alone, at a generous aspect. This is what ships to the favicon (`app/icon.tsx`), the OG card (`app/opengraph-image.tsx`), Twitter share, and eventually a printed business card.
- **Rules:** the mark is ink on paper, always. It never goes on colored backgrounds in Round 1. It never gets a filled color. The circle is a stroke.

## The OG card (1200 × 630)

Composition:

- Full-bleed `--paper` background.
- The standalone mark (ink) at ~96px, top-left, 64px margin.
- "Green Circle" wordmark in Fraunces 500 next to it, baseline-aligned.
- Bottom-third: a single editorial line in Fraunces 500, ~72px, max 3 lines — `"A curated home for the real startups being built in Ethiopia."`
- A single hairline rule above the line, in `--rule`.
- No subtitle, no CTA, no shield, no URL. The feed shouldn't have to read twice.

## Vibe tiles

Three mental anchors we keep pinned next to the Figma tab (or, in our case, the code editor).

1. **A good Sunday magazine.** Generous margins, unhurried, warmly typeset, assumes the reader is smart.
2. **An independently published quarterly.** Small print run, clearly hand-edited, each issue has a date. Issue numbers are a feature, not a gimmick.
3. **A founder's living room.** Real photos on the wall, a kettle on, nothing fabricated. If it wouldn't belong in the room, it doesn't belong on the site.

## What we build in what order (Round 1)

1. **This document.** ← you are here.
2. **Tokens + fonts** ([app/globals.css](../app/globals.css), [app/layout.tsx](../app/layout.tsx)) — so every component below inherits the new language automatically.
3. **Logo refresh + favicon + OG card** ([components/logo/greencirlce-logo.tsx](../components/logo/greencirlce-logo.tsx), new `app/icon.tsx`, new `app/opengraph-image.tsx`) — so the browser tab and the social preview flip to the new identity from day one.
4. **Editorial primitives** (`components/editorial/*`) — `SectionKicker`, `EditorialHeading`, `Rule`, `PullQuote`, `Byline`, `HandpickedMark` (conditional truth-gated).
5. **Homepage components** — hero (no-photo variant default, photo variant when supplied), "What Green Circle is" section, featured startups grid, "How we choose" section, Pulse teaser, footer.
6. **Directory page** — editorial archive, list-style rows, chip filters.
7. **Startup detail page** — magazine profile.
8. **Admin subtitle fix** — one-line swap in [app/admin/adminDash.tsx](../app/admin/adminDash.tsx).
9. **Header mobile drawer** — wire the placeholder.

Each step is a single commit scope. You see the token layer land before any pages move, so you can sanity-check the aesthetic without reading 500 lines of component diff.
